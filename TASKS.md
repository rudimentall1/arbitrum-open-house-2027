## Current priority — find a validated problem, not another speculative idea

- [ ] Identify 5–10 specific potential buyers/users in one narrow workflow and conduct direct problem interviews.
- [ ] Establish whether the problem is frequent, costly, and currently handled with an inadequate workaround.
- [ ] Obtain at least one concrete validation signal (e.g. a design-partner commitment to test a scoped workflow, access to a real workflow/data, or a paid discovery/pilot discussion).
- [ ] Only then compare solution approaches and define an Arbitrum-native MVP with measurable acceptance criteria.

## GO criteria for a concept

A concept cannot move to implementation until all are true:
1. Named ICP and buyer.
2. Specific, recurring, expensive problem supported by primary evidence.
3. Existing workaround and why it fails.
4. At least one credible design partner or equivalent commitment to test.
5. Clear reason the solution needs Arbitrum / its specific capabilities.
6. Demo scope achievable by a small team within the event timeline.
7. Differentiation from existing products demonstrated, not asserted.
